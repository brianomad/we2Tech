A CMS is the system you use to edit your website content. A headless CMS is one where the editing system is separated from the part visitors see, with the two connected through a service that feeds content to any device.

## The difference

With a traditional CMS like WordPress, editing and displaying are tightly tied together. With a headless setup, you can edit once and publish your content to a website, a mobile app and even a kiosk screen, because the content is delivered as data rather than as finished pages.

- You run multiple channels, like a website plus an app, that share the same content

- You need editors in different teams to publish to several places from one place
